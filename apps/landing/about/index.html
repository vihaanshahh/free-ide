About

An editor that stays free because the ad pays.

subspaces.si is a public beta. It is a code-server window with an opencode agent. Flash-class models answer. A sponsored card under the reply covers the tokens. There is no Pro tier to upsell.

On your computer

The folder, the terminal, git, and the edits. You can undo a change the agent made.

On the server

The model call, the token balance, and the check that an ad was actually seen.
